ServiceLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

ServiceLog is an app for independent tradespeople that prepares quotes, work orders and reports. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it stays and which services it is shared with.

Controller

Rony De Sousa, Montevideo, Uruguay. Contact: desousarony@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your data stays on your phone. ServiceLog has no servers of its own and no user accounts, and the developer can't see your clients, quotes or photos.
  • There are no ads, we don't sell data and we don't track you across apps.
  • The app only connects to the services listed below, and none of them receives the content of your documents, except the backup in your own iCloud or Google Drive account if you turn it on.

Data you keep in the app

Your business details, your clients (names, phone numbers, emails and addresses), your catalog, quotes, work orders, notes, photos and signatures are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by the phone's own encryption.

You decide who you share a document with: when you send a PDF, the app opens your phone's share menu and the app you choose (email, WhatsApp or another) sends it. The same applies when you call or message a client.

You are responsible for the client data you enter in the app and for having their consent where the law requires it. We don't access that data.

Permissions

Each permission is requested when it's first needed and you can decline it; the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Camera: to take photos of your work.
  • Photos: to attach photos you already have on your phone.
  • Location (only while using the app): to add where a photo was taken. The coordinates stay on your phone; the app doesn't turn them into an address or send them to any service.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to dictate notes. Recognition happens on the phone; the audio is never sent to a server or stored.
  • Notifications: for agenda reminders. They are local: scheduled on the phone, without going through any server.

Retention and deletion

  • Settings > Privacy and data > Delete all data removes the database, files and scheduled notifications. Uninstalling the app also deletes them from the phone.
  • Backups in iCloud or Google Drive are in your account and you can delete them there. A ZIP backup you export stays wherever you save it.
  • Sentry and TelemetryDeck keep their technical and anonymous data according to their own retention periods.

Children

ServiceLog is designed for professionals and is not directed at minors.

Your rights

Since the app doesn't keep your data on servers of its own, you can view, correct or delete your data directly in the app. For any privacy question, write to desousarony@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll publish the new version on this page with its date. If the change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app.
